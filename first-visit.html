What to expect

A tour, then time to recover.

  1. Buy online or walk in. Buy your first visit online ahead of time and arrive any time during open hours, or buy it at the front desk.
  2. Sign a quick waiver. All first-time visitors sign a brief liability waiver at the front desk. It takes about two minutes.
  3. Take the tour. Every first-time visitor gets a tour, with an explanation of how and why we use each of our amenities.
  4. Recover. A peaceful place where you can truly recover, for as long as you like.
What to bring

Keep it simple.

Bring a swimsuit and a water bottle. Towels are stocked in the wet and sauna area. We have 4 bathrooms; 2 with showers and 2 without.

Safety

New to cold plunge or sauna?

Your walkthrough is tailored to your body, your goals, and any conditions you're managing. If you're pregnant, recovering from a recent injury, or have a cardiovascular condition, please consult your physician beforehand.
