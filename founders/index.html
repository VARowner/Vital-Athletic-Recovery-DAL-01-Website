What's the difference between Founder pricing and standard pricing?

The first 100 members lock in Founder rates for as long as their membership stays active. Standard rates apply to everyone else, including reactivated memberships after a 60-day lapse.

Do the memberships include classes?

Ultimate includes unlimited Reformer and Yoga. Essential and Vital include every recovery modality and discounted class rates — classes are not unlimited on those tiers.

What should I bring?

Swimwear, and workout clothes if you're taking a class. Towels are stocked in the wet and sauna area.
